# Verifier contract

Agree a concrete behavior with the allowlisted operator. The supplied example expects one 10% reduction from 10000 to 9000 cents, plus zero-percent and empty-cart checks. It is authored, not customer data.

Require exact addressed approval in the configured room. Record sender/message identity, freeze the check bundle and reproduce the expected original assertion before dispatching Fixer. Retain the agreement in Switch context.

Validate candidate ancestry, changed files, check digest and runner image. Run the frozen check on original and candidate, followed by adjacent checks, in separate read-only, network-disabled containers. A passing original, missing checks, errors, changed inputs or contradictory replays cannot justify verification.

Post outcome and evidence through Switch. A verified result awaits operator acceptance. Keep unsuccessful attempts and exact evidence available.
