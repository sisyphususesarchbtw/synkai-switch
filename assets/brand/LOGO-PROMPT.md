# NYKT logo generation prompt

Create a distinctive, minimal identity for **NYKT**, a developer tool where humans and AI agents verify bug fixes using executable evidence. The tagline is **Clarity, earned.** The name is coined and has a nocturnal Greek-inspired sound; do not invent Greek text or a translation.

Visual world: dark Zen architecture, basalt stone, quiet negative space, Greek geometric proportion, and a faint echo of an Indian astronomical seal. It must feel precise, contemplative and mysterious, while remaining useful as software branding.

Develop one simple abstract mark using two restrained geometric forms: an incomplete architectural aperture and an offset sliver of light that aligns with it. Suggest a claim becoming observable. The silhouette should be memorable and still readable at 16px. Use confident optical balance, generous negative space and medium-weight forms. Avoid hairlines.

Colors: warm ivory #ECEAE2 and an optional muted brass accent #C0A77A on near-black #101211. Also show a single-color version. Flat geometry only; no gradients, glow, bevels, 3D, stock mockups, ornamental mandalas, deities, literal eyes, moons, generic checkmarks, shields, brains, robots or circuit traces. Do not imitate another company's logo.

Produce: (1) standalone square mark on a transparent background; (2) horizontal lockup with the exact word NYKT in a restrained custom geometric wordmark; (3) small-size favicon test; (4) black-on-white and white-on-black versions. Keep the tagline out of the small logo. Present the identity on one calm contact sheet, then provide the chosen standalone mark separately. If your tool supports actual vector output, provide an editable SVG, not a bitmap embedded in SVG. Otherwise provide a high-resolution transparent PNG and state that it is raster.

Return a brief explanation of the geometry and spacing. Do not claim trademark clearance or fabricate an ancient meaning.

Save generated files in `assets/brand/incoming/` in this repository, or place them in the parent Hackathon folder for inspection. No logo has been generated yet.
